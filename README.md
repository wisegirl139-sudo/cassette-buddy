#cassette buddy
do you like cassettes ? do you have friends ? why not check out cassette buddy!!! now i havent done much backend so this liek a bit of basic backend heres how it works 
you can either use 1 browser or have friends! id reccomend the latter.
then one of you produces a room code and shares it to the other and the other joins.
now the cool bit !! if one of you adds a song you see the others added song , so it alternates , if they added a new song youll see it when you add your song!! and it has a fixed order so u cant skip the horrible bangers!!
now logistically ive used supa base three.js and fusion 360, kinda the odd one out here fusion that 
the 3d cassette is interactable and the songs are stored in supabase(they all do a bit more than that but its alr) 
and alsooo u get cool effects when u play music like floating notes , turning reels and just music with your friendd!
and to pitch it further you can combine it with the cassette mp3 player!! make a play list here and organise ur mpt files them upload them to the mp3 player oliver made!!
